Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

I forgot my password

On the log in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send you a link to choose a new password. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do I change my password?

Open Settings → Change password.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently erases your profile, recipes, cook log, photos, videos and lists right away. It can't be undone, so download your data first if you want a copy.

How do I download my data?

Open Settings → Download your data. You'll get a file with your recipes, cook log, collections, lists and settings.

How do I cancel Master Cook?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → The Cook Club and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your current period ends to avoid being charged again.

How do I report a post or block someone?

Tap Report on the recipe, cook log post, comment or profile, and choose a reason. You can also block the person from their profile. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I make my account private?

Open Settings and turn on Private account. Then only followers you approve can see your recipes and cook log.

Does The Cook Club use AI?

No. Scan a photo reads the words on your own phone, and nothing is sent to an AI company. It can misread a word now and then, so check the recipe before you save it, and always use your own judgment about food safety, cooking temperatures and allergies.

Contact us

Something not working, or have an idea? Email us at contact@jazzformysoul.com, or open Settings → Contact us in the app. We usually reply within 1–2 days.

To report harmful content, please use the Report button in the app so we can see exactly what you mean.
