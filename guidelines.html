Community Guidelines

Last updated October 13, 2026

The Cook Club is a friendly kitchen. Share what you cook, cheer people on, and keep it kind.

Please do

✓ Post your real cooking: recipes, tries, honest ratings and helpful notes.
✓ Give credit when a recipe isn't yours.
✓ Disagree politely. Critique the dish, never the person.

Not allowed

✕ Hate, harassment, bullying or threats.
✕ Sexual or violent content.
✕ Spam, scams, ads, or fake accounts.
✕ Sharing someone's private information.
✕ Dangerous "recipes" or advice that could hurt people.
✕ Content that isn't yours to share.

Words we filter

Before a recipe goes to Home, and when you write a comment, name or bio, the app checks for offensive words. If it finds one, it asks you to change it before it can be shared.

If you see something wrong

Tap Report on the post, comment or profile. You can also Block anyone, and they won't be able to see you or interact with you. We review reports within 24 hours. Posts reported by several people are hidden automatically until we review them.
